## Video 1 — Standalone

**Title:** The Busboy Who Owned the Restaurant

**Description:**
A snobbish food critic humiliates a "busboy" clearing her table — not knowing exactly who he is. When the truth comes out, the tables (literally) turn. A satisfying rich-vs-assumptions twist short about never judging by the uniform. #shorts #plottwist #storytime #karma #viral

**Connection note:** Standalone — no shared characters/visuals with other videos this run.

**Script (narration-ready):**

HOOK:
"She snapped her fingers at him like he was furniture. She had no idea he signed her paycheck."

BEATS:
- Open on ELENA, a sharp-dressed restaurant critic, mid-rant to her assistant about "how hard is it to get risotto right," loud enough for the whole dining room to hear.
- A quiet man in a plain black apron, MARCUS, comes to clear her plate. She snaps her fingers without looking up: "This is cold. Get your manager. Actually — don't. People like you wouldn't understand seasoning anyway."
- Marcus says nothing. He picks up the plate, nods once, and walks toward the kitchen.
- Elena keeps talking to her assistant, name-dropping that her review "could close this place by Friday" — she's done it before.
- The real manager rushes over, pale, apologizing profusely — to MARCUS, not to Elena. "Sir, I'm so sorry, I didn't realize you were seated on the floor tonight—"
- Elena freezes. "Wait. Seated on the floor — what do you mean 'sir'?"
- Marcus finally looks at her, calm: "I own this restaurant. And four others you've reviewed under different names. I like to bus tables on slow Tuesdays — keeps me honest about what guests actually experience."
- He pulls out his phone, shows the reservation system: her table was flagged the moment she walked in, from a photo the staff keeps of "critics to watch."
- Elena, scrambling: "I— the risotto really was cold—"
- Marcus, already walking away with the plate: "It was. I'm having the chef fix it. For everyone else's dinner, not yours. Yours is on the house — as a thank-you for the free lesson in humility."

ENDING:
Final shot: Elena sitting alone as the whole dining room quietly claps for Marcus walking back into the kitchen. Text on screen: "Kindness costs nothing. Assumptions cost everything."

## Video 2 — Part 1 of 2 (connected with Video 3)

**Title:** She Was Fired For "Stealing" — Then Her Boss Found Out Who She Really Was (Part 1)

**Description:**
Single mom Rosa gets publicly accused of theft and fired on the spot by a boss who never bothered to check the facts. Part 1 of 2 — the setup for a payback story that's been building all week. Watch Part 2 for the reveal. #shorts #storytime #revenge #karma #parttwo

**Connection note:** Part 1 of 2, connected with Video 3. Keep ROSA's design (mid-30s, tired eyes, worn cardigan, name tag "ROSA M.") and boss DEREK's design (slicked hair, expensive watch, retail-manager polo) IDENTICAL across both videos — same art style, same store setting (a mid-size retail store, "Lumen Mart").

**Script (narration-ready):**

HOOK:
"He fired her in front of forty customers over $60 missing from the register. He should have checked the cameras first."

BEATS:
- ROSA, exhausted after a double shift, is counting the register at Lumen Mart when her manager DEREK storms up with the till printout.
- Derek, loudly, in front of the line of customers: "Sixty dollars short. Again. You know what that means, Rosa." He doesn't wait for an answer — "Turn in your badge. Now."
- Rosa, stunned: "Derek, I haven't touched anything I shouldn't — can we just check the footage—"
- Derek cuts her off: "I've heard it before. Corporate wants this handled today, not next week." Customers stare; someone films on their phone.
- Rosa quietly takes off her badge, says nothing else, and walks out past the customers who won't meet her eyes — except one, an older woman near the door who watched the whole register count and looks troubled.
- Cut to Rosa that night, at her small kitchen table, counting her own cash for rent while her daughter does homework nearby. She doesn't cry — she just looks tired in a way that's bigger than tonight.
- Text overlay: "She didn't fight it. She didn't have the energy to. But someone else did."
- Final beat: the older customer from the store is shown at home, pulling up something on her laptop — a company directory — and typing an email titled "Re: Incident at Lumen Mart #4471." We don't see the contents yet.

ENDING (cliffhanger):
Text on screen: "She wasn't just a customer. Part 2 — tomorrow." Freeze on the woman hitting send.

## Video 3 — Part 2 of 2 (connected with Video 2)

**Title:** She Was Fired For "Stealing" — Then Her Boss Found Out Who She Really Was (Part 2)

**Description:**
The customer who witnessed Rosa's firing wasn't just anyone — and her email is about to change everything. The payback conclusion to yesterday's story. #shorts #storytime #revenge #karma #parttwo #justice

**Connection note:** Part 2 of 2, connected with Video 2. Same ROSA and DEREK designs and same Lumen Mart setting — must visually match Video 2 exactly (same outfits, same store layout, same "older woman" customer design introduced at the end of Part 1).

**Script (narration-ready):**

HOOK:
"The woman he ignored in the checkout line was the regional VP who'd flown in undercover to audit his store."

BEATS:
- Recap flash (1–2 sec): quick cut back to Derek firing Rosa in front of customers, then snap to "THE NEXT MORNING."
- Derek arrives at work to find two people in suits waiting by his office: HR and a woman he recognizes with a jolt — the "customer" from yesterday, MS. OKAFOR, regional VP.
- Ms. Okafor, calm and precise: "I was in your store yesterday reviewing register-shortage complaints at three locations. Rosa's register wasn't short — the shortage was in the deposit bag from the shift before hers. Your safe log shows it. Did you check it before you fired her in front of forty people?"
- Derek stammers: "Corporate said to move fast—"
- Ms. Okafor: "I'm corporate. And I said no such thing."
- Cut to Rosa at home getting a phone call. She listens, sits down slowly, covers her mouth. Her daughter asks "Mom, what happened?" Rosa, through tears: "I got my job back. With back pay. And... they want me to run the store."
- Final confrontation: Derek is shown clearing his desk while Rosa walks back onto the floor to applause from her coworkers — including the cashier who quietly covered for her that night.
- Ms. Okafor, to Rosa: "I don't reward people for being right. I reward people for staying kind when they had every reason not to."

ENDING:
Final shot: Rosa pinning on a new name tag: "ROSA M. — STORE MANAGER." Text on screen: "Being underestimated is just a head start nobody asked for."
