What we collect, and only when you give it

Nothing is collected from you merely by reading this site. There is no customer account, no login, and no advertising or analytics tracking of any kind. Information reaches us only when you choose to fill in one of two forms.

What we do not collect

  • No analytics. There is no Google Analytics, no Meta pixel, no advertising tag and no visitor-tracking script anywhere on this site.
  • No payment details. Nothing is sold through this website. You never enter a card number, a UPI ID or a bank detail here.
  • No profiling, no selling. We do not build a profile of you, and we do not sell, rent or trade your details to anybody.
  • No location, camera or microphone. The site instructs the browser to refuse these outright.

Your IP address

To stop a robot flooding the booking form, the server counts recent requests against the address they came from. That count lives in the server's memory, is cleared roughly every fifteen minutes, and is never written to a file, never stored alongside your booking, and never used to identify you.

Where your information goes

  • To the shop. Bookings and alert sign-ups are stored on the server running this website, and emailed to primeplay345@gmail.com so the counter knows about them.
  • Google, through Gmail. Our email is sent through Gmail, so Google handles messages we send you, exactly as it handles any other email.
  • Render. This website is hosted by Render, so the server holding your details is theirs.
  • Google Fonts. The page loads two typefaces from Google's font service, which tells Google the IP address of whoever is reading. We receive nothing from this and cannot identify you through it.

That is the complete list. Nobody else receives anything.

How long we keep it

Bookings and alert sign-ups are kept until you ask us to remove them, or until they stop being of any use — an appointment from two years ago is of use to nobody. Rate alerts continue until you tell us to stop.

You can have your details deleted at any time. Write to primeplay345@gmail.com, or send a WhatsApp message to either number above, saying what you want removed. We will do it and tell you when it is done. You need not give a reason.

Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you may ask what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, ask us to erase it, and withdraw a consent you gave earlier. Ask through any contact above. We will not charge you for it.

If you are reading this from the United Kingdom or the European Union and believe the UK or EU GDPR applies to you, the same requests hold and the same contacts work.

Keeping it safe

The site is served over HTTPS. The rate page is behind a password stored only as a scrypt hash, so nobody — including whoever built this site — can read it back. Login sessions are held in a cookie the browser will not hand to scripts, and are never put into ordinary browser storage. No system is perfect and we will not pretend otherwise: if something ever goes wrong with information you gave us, we will tell you.

Children

This site is meant for adults buying jewellery. We do not knowingly collect anything from a child. If you believe a child has sent us their details, write to us and we will delete them.

Changes to this page

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. There is no version history to hunt through — what is written here is what the site does today.
